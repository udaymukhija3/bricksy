# Handoff — Rotation Chess

> **Shipped 2026-09-18 as Mate** — `/mate/`, on the hub, with the daily/endless run loop. Code: `src/lab/mate.ts` + `mate-model.ts` (tested: attacks, mate, legality incl. physical validity, unique answers, answer kind by level). What shipped: attack = shadow minus anchor; states must be physically possible (no shared cells, king's cell clear at ground level); puzzles brute-forced to a unique mating move, forced to a turn, then a horizontal-axis turn, then three pieces; deterministic fallback so a seed never fails; current attacks shown, post-move shadow hidden. The sections below are the original brief; the open questions still stand.

Branch: `chess`. Entry: `/mate/`.

## The spatial concept

Multi-step lookahead over spatial transformations. A piece's attack pattern is
its shape projected onto the board in its current orientation; a move is a
slide or a quarter-turn. To see two moves ahead you must imagine a rotated
pattern that does not yet exist and its consequences for other pieces. It
composes mental rotation with adversarial planning.

## The bypass test

The strategic layer can dominate: a player may win by chess habits without
imagining rotations well. Keep the spatial part load-bearing by making
orientation the *only* thing that changes attack patterns (no piece types
beyond shape), and by using "mate in two" puzzles where the answer is a turn,
not a slide.

## Smallest honest sketch

- 6×6 board, 2–3 pieces per side, each a tri- or tetracube whose footprint in
  its orientation (cells at y = 0 projected onto the board, relative to the
  anchor) is its attack set.
- Puzzle mode, not an opponent: "find the move that leaves the red piece with
  no safe square" — commit a move; the board reveals attack maps after.
- Model: `attacks(piece, orientation)` from `polycube.ts`; puzzles generated
  by brute force over one- and two-move sequences (tiny state space).

## What it must prove

Whether people can plan a *turn* (not a slide) as a chess-like move at all,
and whether accuracy on "the answer is a rotation" puzzles tracks pack.

## Why it wasn't sketched yet

Needs a puzzle corpus (generated by search) and a rules pass; rendering is
trivial. Lowest priority of the four concepts; highest ceiling if it works.
